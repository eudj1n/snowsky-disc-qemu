## Reusable findings

MIPS user-mode setup, zlog-assisted symbol recovery, tinyalsa card discovery, the
guest-accessible mount-source gate, classified key gestures, and a202 event/reply
ambiguity are useful leads for later versions. Their **addresses and exact layouts**
are V2.40-specific until revalidated; follow [PORTING](../PORTING.md), not a blind patch copy.

`PRODUCT=SNOWSKY_DISC`, Buildroot 2020.02.1 and `/home/FiiO/work/ECHO/fiio_music_mvc/`
strings were observed in V2.40 metadata/binaries. ECHO is an internal project/platform
identifier candidate, not a confirmed official OS brand or proof of other-device support.
